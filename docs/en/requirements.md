# Requirements baseline

[中文](../../requirements.md) | [Home](../../README.en.md)

This consolidated English baseline reflects the latest confirmed requirements; dated Chinese entries remain historical context.

1. Support all standard-product categories and only new, genuine goods. Manufacturers, authorized agents, independent distributors, traders and platform sellers are eligible channel categories; seller assertions alone do not verify authenticity.
2. Collect one item in chat and multiple items through Excel. If complete input already exists, do not ask the count again. Require product name, brand, complete model, positive quantity, unit, destination country/region and city for each task. An explicit unbranded/no-model request needs unique specifications. Due date and other procurement conditions are optional.
3. Search mainland China per request first. Expand globally only when no mainland channel simultaneously meets exact model/specifications, quantity, authenticity and procurement conditions. A qualified mainland channel awaiting a price does not alone trigger global search.
4. Record seller identity, product URL, timestamp, inventory evidence, authenticity/authorization evidence, price, currency, packaging, MOQ, purchase multiples, quantity breaks, taxes, freight, origin and lead time. Distinguish inventory from lead time, inbound stock from current stock, and public listings from supplier confirmations.
5. Never invent unknown quantities or prices. Preserve inaccessible pages, contradictory data and unverified claims with their limitations. No login, supplier contact or ordering is performed automatically.
6. Convert packaging and select the price tier applicable to required quantity. Rank only qualified comparable quotes per item/tax group by unit price. Do not combine suppliers unless explicitly permitted. Alternative products remain separate from exact-match rankings.
7. Keep original currency and source price. Foreign conversion requires a real FX source and date; reference CNY is not landed cost. Unknown freight, tax or duty is not zero.
8. Group missing data and decisions by stable request ID/source row. Valid items continue while incomplete ones remain visible. Up to five questions use chat; more use a decision workbook with blank responses. Partial consent stays partial; silence and “continue” do not grant consent.
9. Export the three sheets described in the user guide. Keep all requests, including no-result/pending ones, and all relevant quotes with reasons. Deduplicate repeated channel/product/condition records without hiding conflicts. All table headers and cells are centered horizontally/vertically; row heights fit wrapped content.
10. Deliver portable rules, blank template and helpers without private business data, credentials, local absolute paths or dependencies. Target Codex, WorkBuddy, Doubao desktop, Claude Code, Comate and Qianwen desktop chat; Qwen Code is optional and distinct. Do not equate documentation or copying with client acceptance.

Acceptance includes valid Excel, mandatory destination validation, stable IDs, partial batches, per-item global expansion, correct packaging/tier/unknown handling, source traceability, honest no-result reporting and separate records for simulated versus real/client tests.
