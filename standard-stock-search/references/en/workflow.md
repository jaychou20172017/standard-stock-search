# Search workflow

[中文](../workflow.md)

Process requests by ID across standard-product categories. Explicit unbranded/no-model input requires sufficiently unique specifications; clarify missing identity rather than infer it.

Search mainland China using full manufacturer part number, brand and stock/price terms. Cover accessible manufacturer/authorized, distribution and platform listings; record actual queries, scope, timestamp and obstacles. Stop when successive queries add no verifiable leads. A fixed number of hits does not establish completeness.

Check full suffix, version, dimensions, packaging and other key specifications on product pages. Identify actual sellers. Verify authorization through manufacturer or other reliable evidence; seller claims alone stay pending. Public stock is not a supply commitment and lead time is not inventory.

Use the quantity tier that applies to the requested quantity, record its range, convert pack prices/stock/MOQ/multiples to request units and do not silently overbuy. Keep unknown fees and tax unknown. A mainland channel meeting supply requirements but awaiting price does not by itself trigger global expansion.

If no mainland channel meets exact identity, quantity, originality and procurement conditions, search globally for that item. Verify delivery to destination and requested deadline; unknown delivery/date remains pending. A complete search may yield no qualified quote.

Use only obtained FX values with source/date, defined as CNY per original currency unit. A converted reference excludes unknown tax, freight and duty and is not landed cost. Different tax bases rank separately.

Record evidence for every true flag. Merge repeated product/seller/condition records but preserve distinct tiers and conflicts; do not choose favorable snapshots from conflicting sources. Check coverage for every searched request before final JSON/export.
